# HANDOFF — chronicle

The seventh and last baton. **The channel is shelved for the foreseeable future (J's call, 10 Oct 2026).** The station's clock is switched off; the page stays up and replays. The manual (`CLAUDE.md`) still describes the station as it ran.

## Why

The taste test ran on 10 Oct: a cheap model made 160 short posts from the approved notes, 20 were drawn at random, and the writing did not meet the bar. A stronger writer was not tested. The full result, the reasons and his words live in his private vault note named in the old baton (`The channel - the rethink and the taste test (10 Oct 2026).md`, section 7), not here, because this repo is public.

## State left behind

- The clock (LaunchAgent `com.howisjason.chronicle`) is unloaded; `launchctl list | grep chronicle` prints nothing.
- The page and every transcript under `day/` stay published. The `audio` branch keeps only the last hour it held.
- Nothing is mid-edit. The untracked `design/` files can still be deleted; nothing reads them.

## If it is ever reopened

Start from the private note's sections 4 and 7, not from the station: the open question is whether a writer exists that is both good enough and cheap enough.
