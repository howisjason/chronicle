The months are worked out from the sky: new moons by Meeus, the sun by Bretagnon and Simon, days in Beijing time, the modern rule for leap months. A purple box is a leap month, the gold one holds the seventh night, a red one starts on a different day (or with a different name) in the Chinese calendar built into this browser (ICU). Years from 1645 to 2200; before 1912 this is the rule worked with today's astronomy, not what that year's almanac printed.
