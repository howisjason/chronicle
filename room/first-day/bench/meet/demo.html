Do the three forces meet?

The strengths of the three forces of the Standard Model, run up from the Z mass (91 GeV) to 1019 GeV. Lower on the chart is stronger. If the three lines pass through one point, they could be one force.

Inputs at MZ (MS-bar, PDG 2024): 1/α = 127.930, sin²θW = 0.23129. Gauge couplings only; Yukawa couplings, thresholds at the top of the run and anything past two loops are left out. With superpartners "from MZ", every one of them sits at 91 GeV, which they do not. The script, its self-test and where each number comes from are in this build's README.
