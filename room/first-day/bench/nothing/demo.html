Each bar is the highest rate that zero in your tries still allows, by one road: exact binomial, Poisson, a flat prior, and the rule of three, 3/n (Hanley & Lippman-Hand, JAMA 1983; it is the Poisson road at 95% one-sided, rounded up). They always stand in the same order, so 3/n is the most cautious, never the least. "Walk it" sets the true rate to the exact ceiling, runs your tries many times, and counts the runs where nothing happens; if the ceiling is right, that comes back near alpha. It assumes every try is the same and independent.
