Is your browser's Chinese calendar fixed yet?

daythe Observatoryyour browser

Each row is the first day of a month as the Observatory's table prints it, in a year where ICU, the calendar library inside most browsers, has been seen to start that month on another day. All twelve come down to a new moon or the sun crossing a mark within a quarter hour of midnight in Beijing. ICU knows: ICU-23469, ICU-13195, ICU-23274. The tracker gives ICU 79.1, listed for 15 October 2026, as the fix version, but on 15 October the branch 79.1 ships from did not hold the fix (#4070 was still open). Browsers take a new ICU some weeks or months after it ships. Open this page again to see whether yours has.
