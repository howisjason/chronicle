The Higgs is:

A Higgs decays to a Z and a light particle, which splits in two. Each dot is the softer half of one decay, placed where it lands as seen from the harder half (sideways is angle φ around the beam, up is pseudorapidity η). The circle is a jet of radius 0.4: a dot inside means the two halves are swept into one jet. The mass lights up inside the 0.5 to 3.5 GeV window ATLAS searched (arXiv 2411.16361). A toy: no detector, no showering.
