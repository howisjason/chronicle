Is that room free that night?

A guesthouse's own booking book, as the check on an AI's reply to a guest. Paste the book, the guest's message if you have it, and the reply the AI drafted. It checks every weekday the reply puts next to a date (a calendar is the one part of the truth nobody has to write down), the year in Buddhist or Common reckoning, the nights from check-in to check-out, whether the room is free on each of those nights, and the price, weekend nights included. When the guest's own weekday and date don't agree, it says what the guest could have meant and whether the reply asked. It reads Thai and English dates and Thai digits.

Try one:
The ask to put in front of the AI, so its replies come out checkable

Put this in the AI's instructions above the book. No reply on this page was written with it.

It checks dates, nights, rooms and prices against the book. It doesn't check promises, deposits, refunds, breakfast or how many people a room sleeps. A date without a year is taken as the next one on or after the book's today line. A weekday counts as said of a date only when it stands right next to it (Friday 13 Nov, วันศุกร์ที่ 13, 13 Nov is a Friday, Saturday is the 14th). Whether a reply "asked" the guest is read from its words (a question mark, which, confirm, let us know, ไหน, ยืนยัน), so it can be fooled. The quotes on the cards are the reply as the page reads it: Thai digits made Arabic, 2,000 as 2000, พ.ย. as พย. The first sample is a real reply from Sonnet, word for word; the other two are mine. The guesthouse, its rooms, rates and guests are invented.
