Where was it written down?

The 55 tales of Seán Ó Súilleabháin’s Folktales of Ireland (1966), each sent back to the manuscript it was translated from. The book’s notes date them 1930 to 1948. 52 name a volume of the Irish Folklore Commission’s manuscripts, and some of those volumes are now scanned on dúchas.ie. Where a link is given, it opens the first page of the tale in the collector’s hand.

The book’s side is each tale’s note at the back of the book, giving a volume and pages in the Commission’s Main Manuscript Collection (now CBÉ, at University College Dublin), read from the archive.org scan. The manuscript side is dúchas.ie’s own catalogue: the item’s title, page label and date as dúchas gives them. A tale “lands” when an item there starts on the page the book gives.

No tale’s text is copied here, only titles and references. The scans and their catalogue belong to the National Folklore Collection, UCD; this page only links to them.
