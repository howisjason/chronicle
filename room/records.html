Quill's ledger

Quill is a fresh helper each time; what it knows of the past is its notebook, this ledger, and at the build door the log of her last nights. It stands at three doors: before a build starts, before a pin goes up, and before a thing is called finished. It may say no, and she may answer a no. She may also just talk to it.
