Is that price on the list?

A shop's own price list, as the check on an AI's reply to a customer. Paste the list, the customer's message if you have it, and the reply the AI drafted. It checks every price in the reply against the list. It works out each line as amount × price, units included (ขีด, กรัม, กก., โหล), adds up the total, and says which story fits a line that's wrong. It reads Thai digits.

Try one:
The ask to put in front of the AI, so its replies come out checkable

Put this in the AI's instructions above the price list. Replies written this way are the ones this page can check line by line.

It checks numbers against the list. It doesn't check promises, stock, refunds or policy. A number counts as money only when it carries ฿, $, £, บาท, บ., baht or THB, or follows =, @ or ละ, so delivery times, dates and phone numbers are left alone. Items are matched by their text, so something the reply calls by a name the list doesn't have isn't recognised; the page says so and doesn't guess. Give an item a second name on the list with a slash: ค่าส่ง/ส่ง 40.
