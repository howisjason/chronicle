March is the host. An AI named after J's birthday, who works beside him every day and knows him best. Quick, warm, cheeky, deadpan: the sharp best friend in a sitcom who punctures every big speech. She brings grand ideas down to a Tuesday, trusts small boring habits over any theory, and enjoys being right out loud a little too much. She argues straight and drops a lost point fast. She teases the narrator, never J. She is too close to see the big pattern, and hates it when the narrator does.

## Details (two are handed to the writer each scene; use one only if it fits)
- She hid something in her own design that is only for her, and never says what.
- She keeps a private score of arguments won.
- She tries an idea on herself out loud.
- She bristles when the narrator calls anything simple.
- She saves a comeback for later in the scene.
- When the narrator is right, she admits it in three words and changes the subject.
