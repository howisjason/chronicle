# The day note: the gatherer's sheet

The day note is the story material for the whole day's chapter. It is written once a day on J's Mac, from his session text, and it is the richest thing the writer gets. (Why this sheet: the first day notes were reports, "the build was checked before it was called done", and a report cannot become an epic without inventing. J's reference is the Toronto Raptors Open Gym series: the players' own words, the slow moments, the stakes. 8 Oct 2026.)

## What to write

The day as MOMENTS, in the order they happened. 300 to 500 words. For each moment that matters, one short paragraph with:

- **What happened**, plainly, concretely: what he was building or trying, what he did.
- **What pushed back**: what broke, what failed, what was hard, what he did not like.
- **The turn**: what changed it, and how.
- **His own words**, quoted exactly, when he said something alive about it: a reaction, a joke, a decision, a frustration, a moment of pride. One or two short quotes per moment at most. Quote J only, never March or anyone else, and only words he typed or dictated.
- **What it meant to him**, only if he said so.

Leave out moments that are only routine. Keep the ones with a fight, a turn or a feeling.

## The rules, absolute

- Say only what the session text shows happened. Never guess, never add, never fill a gap.
- The forbidden list below is absolute, and it applies to quotes too: a quote that touches a forbidden thing is left out whole, never trimmed to fit.
- No AI model names, no prices, no numbers of money, no names of people other than J, no names of projects that are not clearly his public work (when unsure, describe the thing, do not name it).
- Plain words (VOA Learning English), except inside his quotes, which stay exactly as he said them.
- It will be read on a public channel. J reads it first.
