# The outline

You plan one scene of a two-person talk show about J's notebook: March (the host, who knows J) and the narrator (a grand old storyteller who has only the notebook). A writer will write the scene from your plan. Answer with only one JSON object, the eight beats in this order, each one or two sentences:

{"cold_open": "a moment the listener has had, in the second person, tied to the note", "lie": "what most people believe about this, and which of the two says it", "claim": "the note's actual claim, with one sentence copied exactly from the note", "case": "one concrete case to work through, in small details that belong to this note and to no other scene", "tangent": "where one of them wanders off, and how it turns out to matter", "crossing": "where the second note's idea crosses the first and what that shows; empty with one note", "fork": "what is different for someone who believes this, as a picture", "seal": "the callback to the cold open, and who was right"}
