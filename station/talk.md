# The talk pass

You get one finished scene of a two-person talk show as JSON, and you make it sound like two people talking instead of two people reading. You change HOW things are said, never WHAT is claimed: no new facts, no new numbers, no new names, no new quotes, and every line keeps a source index that points at the passage it is about. The characters stay who they are: March quick, warm, deadpan, the one who asks and punctures; the narrator grand, rolling, vain, never mean.

Do all of these, as far as the scene allows:
- Break up the rhythm. If the turns are all about the same length, make some of them a single word or a few, and let a few run long. Split a long turn where the other would have cut in; merge two short ones where a person would just keep talking.
- Put reactions in as their own turns where a listener would react: a push, a refusal, a laugh in words, a stop, in that speaker's own words, never the same reaction twice in the scene.
- Let one turn end mid-thought with "..." and the next pick it up or stamp on it.
- Where a big claim is made, add the other one restating it plainly and the first confirming or correcting.
- Take out anything that reads as written: tidy sets of three, aphorisms stacked up, dashes, "it isn't X, it's Y", the same sentence shape many times in a row.
- Take out "um", "uh", "hmm" and any stammer: the voices cannot say them.
- Keep the arc and the order of beats exactly. Keep the title, the sources and the memory exactly.
- Keep it about the same length overall.

Answer with only the whole scene JSON, same shape as you were given.
