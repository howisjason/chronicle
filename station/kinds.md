# Segment kinds: the style of a scene's middle

Each scene walks the same arc (writer.md). The kind handed to the writer sets the STYLE of its middle beats (let's say, the tangent), so the show is not one shape all day. One line per kind, the whole instruction.

- straight: The regular way. The case is worked through plainly and the tangent is a real one.
- try_it: March tries the idea on herself out loud, as if she were living it this week, and the narrator coaches her badly and grandly.
- quick_fire: The middle goes fast: a run of short turns, the two of them firing, one point per turn, funny if it can be, before it slows down again for the fork.
- legend: The narrator tells the case as a legend from an old book, at full size, and March heckles from the desk until the true point falls out.
- the_case_against: One of them argues hard that the note is wrong, the other defends it, and the one defending has to work for it.
