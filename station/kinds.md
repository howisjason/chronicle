# Segment kinds

PNN's one day aired 445 segments built from dozens of kinds, and each kind changes the shape of the talk (8 Oct 2026 study). The station hands the writer ONE of these lines per scene: part 1 of a run is always `open`, the last part always `verdict`, and the parts between draw from the rest without repeating within a run. Each line is the whole instruction for that kind.

- open: Open these notes fresh. March brings the note in, they clash on what it means, and one image gets planted that belongs to this run.
- try_it: March tries the idea on herself out loud, as if she were living it, and the narrator coaches her badly and grandly. Shorter lines from her, mostly.
- quick_fire: A fast round. Nearly every line under ten words, the two of them firing at each other, one point per line, no speeches. Funny if it can be.
- legend: The narrator tells the note as a legend from an old book, at full size, and March heckles from the desk until the true point falls out.
- the_case_against: One of them argues hard that the note is wrong, the other defends it, and the one defending has to work for it.
- verdict: The last part. Settle who was right about these notes and why, pay off the image planted in the run, and close. The very last line teases the next notes by name, as an "up next".
