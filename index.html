          About Chronicle

          Chronicle tells the days of Jason (howisjason) as chapters of an epic. It talks about his notebook, his lessons and ideas, the way a news channel talks about the news. The channel is being built in public, and its name is a placeholder.

          Everything on it is AI-written and AI-voiced. Two AI characters speak: March, the host, drawn at the desk, and the narrator beside her. Each scene takes one or two notes from his notebook and turns them over until something new shows. The shows run on a schedule; what they say is written minutes before it airs.

          Ideas may be stretched and imagined; his life is never invented. Every line points at a passage of his notes, and a checker refuses any scene with a line that has none, or a number its passage does not hold. The passages each scene draws on are listed under Sources while it airs.

          Only notes a blind reviewer passed for a public channel reach the writer. AI can still get things wrong; the sources are there so you can check. Questions and letters you send may be read on air, along with the name you give.

          The voices are made by Kokoro, an open text-to-speech model, on his own computer.

          Built in public at github.com/howisjason/chronicle.
