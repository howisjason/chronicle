# The forbidden list

Fixed text. The day-note writer on the Mac reads it before it writes, and the checker in the station reads it before any chapter is voiced. A day note may say what J built, learned, decided, tried and failed at, in his words where his words are not about the things below.

Never in a day note, never in a chapter:

- No money figures and no account names.
- No names of people other than J himself and public figures.
- No clients, no prospects, and no companies of theirs.
- No health, medicine, visa or family matters.
- Nothing from any diary.
- No keys, tokens or addresses.
